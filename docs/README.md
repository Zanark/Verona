# Verona engineering book

**Status:** foundation and research manuscript, not a finished product book or
rendered PDF. The speech-driven-face idea is recorded; proposed architecture
and technology choices are distinguished from implemented behavior. A bounded
numerical CPU inference tool, complete local text-to-motion generator and
audio-clocked browser player exist. Chapter 06 records their actual mechanisms,
real-browser qualification and still-unaccepted articulation limits.
Chapter 07 extends that baseline with editable Hazel, native Claire/hair and
measured caching/performance mechanisms.
Chapter 08 supersedes its presentation topology with native quad contours,
shaded features, static cosmetic eyes and measured 60-fps display qualification.
Chapter 09 adds Verona's original rest styling and layered wavy hair, then
records the owner's correction restoring the cool polygon default with an
optional warm shaded view. Claire motion and measured display boundaries remain.
Chapter 10 adds the local desktop shell, genuinely transparent pop-out, bounded
cursor gaze and independent XYZ orientation without duplicating speech.
Its vr-14 extension adds optional shoulder-free head/neck framing, retained
full-bust extent and actual polygon/animated-normal work reduction.
Chapter 09's vr-15 extension records the supplied-reference color study,
bounded eyelid/rest changes, procedural cosmetics and intentional color pixel
density cap. It is not a photorealistic reconstruction or likeness acceptance.
The owner rejected that study's likeness. Chapter09 now also records the
explicitly deferred custom-avatar roadmap (vr-16 through vr-19); those plans
do not implement a replacement model, importer or facial rig.
The vr-20 extension preserves Claire and adds FaceMesh, FaceDot and
UniformPolygons over the same motion, with detail/occlusion/performance contracts
in chapter09. The future GLB identity is called Realistic and remains deferred.

## How this becomes a book

The chapters above are the canonical manuscript, not disposable notes to be
rewritten at release. Add source-linked architecture, data/algorithms, design
principles, subsystems, UI, quality and delivery chapters only as those subjects
exist. Each implemented workstream updates its affected chapter in the same
working slice and records the causal story in the development ledger.

When a rendered edition is requested, assemble only the explicit manifest
allowlist, render diagrams locally, generate reading HTML and native PDF, then
inspect actual output and freeze a versioned edition receipt. No arbitrary page
quota, copied private diary, placeholder chapter padding or automatic publication.
